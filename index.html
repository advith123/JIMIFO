How Faze AI works

It reads the page, then shows its working

Three steps, in order — because the order is what keeps the answer traceable back to a number you can check.

STEP 01

Reads what is on the page

Statements, ratios, ownership, corporate actions, coverage and news for that one company. Nothing else enters the answer.

STEP 02

Weighs five factors

Valuation, growth, profitability, balance sheet and momentum are scored and weighted into a single reading out of 100.

Ask about margins, risks or the analyst spread. Answers quote the figure they came from, and stop short of telling you to buy.

Questions

Before you sign up

What is the difference between Faze One and Faze AI?

The company pages are the same. Faze One shows the reported and derived figures and leaves the interpretation to you. Faze AI adds a written reading of those figures, the five-factor Jimifo score, plain-language explainers on each metric, a tone read on headlines, and a chat that answers from the page.

Is Faze One really free?

Yes. Faze One is free to use with no expiry and no card required — the full company page, every section, for every covered company. Faze AI is the paid plan, and it is the only thing you are ever asked to pay for.

What happens if I open a Faze AI feature on the free plan?

You are taken to the pricing page rather than into the feature. The written reading, the Jimifo score, the metric explainers, the headline tone read and the chat all stay locked on Faze One — the company data itself never does.

Does Jimifo tell me what to buy?

No. The Jimifo score is a reading of the data on a page, not a recommendation, and it carries no price forecast. Ask the chat whether to buy something and it will hand you the relevant figures instead of an instruction.

Which markets are covered?

Indian listed equities on the NSE and BSE only. There is no coverage of any other asset class, and none is planned.

Can I switch between plans?

Yes, at any time from the plans page. Moving up to Faze AI takes effect immediately. Moving back down to Faze One takes effect at the end of the period you have paid for, and Faze One stays available to you free after that.

Is the data in this preview real?

No. Every figure across this preview is illustrative sample data built to demonstrate the interface, not a record of any real trading session.
